# Cadence as currency
Teams trade on predictability. A clean weekly cadence is worth more than a flashy roadmap.

## Signals to watch
- Deployment health without babysitting.
- Burn plotted next to roadmap risk.

## Decision hygiene
If a decision cannot be explained in a paragraph, it is not ready.
