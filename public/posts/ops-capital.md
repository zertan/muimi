# Operational capital for builders

A minimalist toolkit for measuring and extending execution runway without drowning in dashboards.

## Cadence as currency

Teams trade on predictability. A clean weekly cadence is worth more than a flashy roadmap that never ships.

## Signals to watch

- Deployment health without babysitting.
- Burn plotted next to roadmap risk.
- Lead time aligned with staffing reality.

## Decision hygiene

If a decision cannot be explained in a paragraph, it is not ready. Write it down, time-box it, and test the assumption with a small release.
